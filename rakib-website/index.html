Md Refat Jahan Rakib

Graduate Research and Teaching Assistant · School of Ocean, Earth and Coastal Science, The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley

Chemical and Marine Oceanographer · UTRGV

I am a chemical and marine oceanographer specializing in emerging pollutants — microplastics, nanoplastics, heavy metals, and marine debris. My research focuses on understanding the distribution, fate, and ecological risks of these pollutants in aquatic and coastal ecosystems, and developing eco-friendly remediation strategies.

I work as a Graduate Research and Teaching Assistant at the School of Ocean, Earth, and Coastal Science, The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley. My research has been published in Science of The Total Environment, Marine Pollution Bulletin, Scientific Reports, Journal of Hazardous Materials, and TrAC Trends in Analytical Chemistry, with 4,000+ citations on Google Scholar. I am a member of the American Chemical Society (ACS) and the American Geophysical Union (AGU).

Beyond research, I am passionate about ocean conservation. I volunteer with the Surfrider Foundation at UTRGV, organizing beach cleanups along the Texas Gulf Coast.
